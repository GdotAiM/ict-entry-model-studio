# ICT entry models
- `references/models.md` — core sequence, ICT 2022 model, Unicorn: what each needs, entry, stop, invalidation.
- `references/charter-models.md` — Charter Price Action Models 1–13 and the five-stage plan.
- `references/charter-detection.md` — which Charter checks are mechanical, approximated, or impossible here.
- `references/glossary.md` — terms. Use only these; do not invent ICT terms.

Every alert names **one** model (the most specific that fits) and the **Charter stage**:
| Stage | When |
|---|---|
| 1 Preparation | Morning: bias, dealing range, liquidity map, calendar |
| 2 Opportunity Discovery | A level was raided, MSS not confirmed yet ("watching") |
| 3 Trade Planning | Setup valid, limit order idea pending |
| 4 Trade Execution | The limit filled |
| 5 Trade Management | TP1 / breakeven decision / TP2 / stop |
